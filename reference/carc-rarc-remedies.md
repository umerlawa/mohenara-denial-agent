# CARC / RARC Codes, Group Codes, and Remedies

> Retrieved 2026-08-12. Every rule below carries a primary-source citation.
>
> **Retrieval note:** direct HTTP fetching of `x12.org`, `cms.gov`, `ecfr.gov` and every other
> host was blocked by this environment's network egress proxy. Rule text below was retrieved
> via domain-restricted search over those same primary domains, plus government
> republications of the X12 lists (state Medicaid agencies, DoD/TRICARE manuals, CMS
> transmittals). **X12 is the controlling authority for code meanings**
> ([X12, Claim Adjustment Reason Codes](https://x12.org/codes/claim-adjustment-reason-codes);
> [X12, Remittance Advice Remark Codes](https://x12.org/codes/remittance-advice-remark-codes);
> [X12, External Code Lists](https://x12.org/codes)) — where the wording below is a retrieved
> excerpt rather than a verbatim read of the live list, that is recorded in **Open questions**.
>
> **Decision warning:** the CARC never determines who owes the balance. The **group code**
> does. A CARC read without its group code cannot answer "can this be billed to the patient".
> See §2 before disposing of any balance.

## 1. Authority, and what each artifact is

| Artifact | Maintained by | X12 External Code Source | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CARC (Claim Adjustment Reason Code) | X12 | **139** | States *why* a claim or line was paid differently than billed. If there is no adjustment, there is no CARC. | [X12, CARC list](https://x12.org/codes/claim-adjustment-reason-codes); [X12, External Code Lists](https://x12.org/codes) |
| RARC (Remittance Advice Remark Code) | X12 | **411** | *Supplemental* RARCs add detail to an adjustment already described by a CARC. *Informational* RARCs ("Alert:") convey remittance information and are **never** tied to a specific adjustment or CARC. | [X12, RARC list](https://x12.org/codes/remittance-advice-remark-codes) |
| CAGC (Claim Adjustment Group Code), 835 `CAS01` | X12 (internal list, 835 TR3) | n/a | Assigns **financial responsibility** for the adjusted dollars. | [CMS, Medicare Claims Processing Manual Ch. 22](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c22.pdf) |
| CORE-required code combinations | CAQH CORE (operating rule under ACA administrative simplification) | n/a | Constrains which CAGC/CARC/RARC combinations a health plan may send for four defined denial business scenarios. Updated 3x/year. | [CAQH CORE, Payment & Remittance (835) Uniform Use of CARCs and RARCs Rule](https://www.caqh.org/hubfs/43908627/drupal/core/Payment-Remittance-CARCs-RARCs-Rule.pdf) |

Payer obligation when denying: the payer must report the CARC **and** the group code that
accurately identify both the reason for the denial and the proper responsibility for the
denied amount — a blanket `CAS*CO*45` full write-off is not an accurate business statement.
Source: [X12 RFI #1802, CO45 Adjust as a full writeoff](https://x12.org/resources/requests-for-interpretation/rfi-1802-co45-adjust-full-writeoff);
[X12 RFI #2052, CAS*CO*45 being used as denial](https://x12.org/resources/requests-for-interpretation/rfi-2052-casco45-being-used-denial).

## 2. Group codes — who owes the balance

This table is the gate. Run it **before** the CARC table.

| Group code | Meaning | Who the balance falls on | Billable to patient? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CO** | Contractual Obligations. "Shall be used when a contractual agreement between the payer and payee, or a regulatory requirement, resulted in an adjustment." | **Provider** | **No.** CO assigns responsibility to the provider. | [CMS, Claims Processing Manual Ch. 22](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c22.pdf) |
| **PR** | Patient Responsibility. | **Patient** | **Yes**, subject to the overrides in §3. Medicare beneficiaries "may be billed only when Group Code PR is used with an adjustment." | [CMS, Claims Processing Manual Ch. 22](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c22.pdf) |
| **OA** | Other Adjustments. "Shall be used when no other group code applies." | Neither party is asserted; liability is **not stated** | **No** — OA does not assign patient liability. Treat as unresolved and pursue the underlying reason (wrong payer, duplicate, COB). | [CMS, Claims Processing Manual Ch. 22](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c22.pdf) |
| **PI** | Payer Initiated Reductions. Used when, in the payer's opinion, the adjustment is **not** the patient's responsibility but **there is no supporting contract** between provider and payer. | Payer-asserted reduction with no contractual basis | **No**, and the provider is not contractually bound by it — PI is the strongest signal to appeal rather than write off. | [MagnaCare 835 (005010X221A1) Companion Guide](https://www.magnacare.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/MagnacareCompanionGuide_835_5010A1.pdf); [X12 RFI #2052](https://x12.org/resources/requests-for-interpretation/rfi-2052-casco45-being-used-denial) ("dollars were not assigned due to no contract between the payer and provider (PI)") |
| **CR** | Corrections and Reversals. | n/a — reversal/correction bookkeeping only | No | [CMS, Claims Processing Manual Ch. 22](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c22.pdf) |

### 2a. Payer-type differences on group codes — do not generalise

| Payer type | Permitted group codes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Medicare FFS (A/B MACs, DME MACs)** | **CO, CR, OA, PR only.** "CMS has never permitted Medicare contractors to use this group code [PI] as it fails to identify financial liability for the unpaid amount." **PI may not be used for Medicare.** | [CMS, Claims Processing Manual Ch. 22](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c22.pdf) |
| **Medicaid (FFS and MCO)** | All X12 group codes are technically valid; state Medicaid programs are observed sending CO and PI. Patient liability is separately constrained by 42 CFR 447.15 (§3). | [X12 RFI #2052](https://x12.org/resources/requests-for-interpretation/rfi-2052-casco45-being-used-denial) (state Medicaid payer sending `CO*45` as a denial); [42 CFR 447.15](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-C/part-447/subpart-A/section-447.15) |
| **Commercial / HIPAA-covered health plans** | All X12 group codes including **PI**, but combinations for the four CORE-defined denial scenarios are constrained by the CORE 360 operating rule. | [CAQH CORE 360 Rule](https://www.caqh.org/hubfs/43908627/drupal/core/Payment-Remittance-CARCs-RARCs-Rule.pdf) |

## 3. Overrides — when a PR balance still cannot be billed to the patient

Apply these **after** reading the group code. Each one defeats a PR assignment.

| Condition | Rule | Effect on the balance | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patient is a **QMB** (Qualified Medicare Beneficiary) | "Federal law prohibits all Original Medicare and MA providers and suppliers (not only those that accept Medicaid) from billing QMBs for Part A and Part B cost sharing" — deductibles, coinsurance, copayments. RA carries **N781** (no deductible may be collected) and **N782** (no coinsurance may be collected); "review your records for any wrongfully collected" amounts. | **Do not bill patient.** Bill Medicaid/secondary. Refund anything collected. | [CMS MLN, Prohibition on Billing Qualified Medicare Beneficiaries](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/mln7936176-prohibition-billing-qualified-medicare-beneficiaries.pdf); [CMS, QMB Remittance Advice Issue (MM9911 update)](https://www.cms.gov/Medicare-Medicaid-Coordination/Medicare-and-Medicaid-Coordination/Medicare-Medicaid-Coordination-Office/Downloads/MM9911Update112017.pdf) |
| Provider participates in **Medicaid**, service covered by the State plan | State plan must limit participation to providers who accept, as payment in full, the amount paid by the agency plus any plan-required deductible/coinsurance/copayment. | **Do not balance-bill** the Medicaid beneficiary beyond State-plan cost sharing. | [42 CFR 447.15](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-C/part-447/subpart-A/section-447.15) |
| Claim is subject to **No Surprises Act** protections | RARC **N830**: "…any amount identified with OA, CO, or PI cannot be collected from the member and may be considered provider liability or be billable to a subsequent payer. Any amount the provider collected over the identified PR amount must be refunded to the patient within applicable Federal/State timeframes." RARCs effective 2026-11-01; required for items/services furnished on or after 2027-01-01. | Only the **PR** amount is collectable; refund overages. | [CMS/CCIIO, Guidance on Required Remittance Advice Remark Codes (No Surprises Act)](https://www.cms.gov/cciio/programs-and-initiatives/other-insurance-protections/caa-nsa-rarc-codes.pdf) |
| Medicare **medical-necessity** denial with **no** valid ABN (modifier **GZ**) | Contractors deny with Group Code **CO** (provider/supplier liable) and CARC 50. | **Provider liable. Do not bill patient.** | [CMS Pub 100-04 transmittal R2148CP](https://www.cms.gov/Regulations-and-Guidance/Guidance/Transmittals/downloads/R2148CP.pdf) |
| Medicare medical-necessity denial **with** signed ABN on file (modifier **GA**) | Group Code **PR** assigns liability to the beneficiary when a claim is received with GA indicating a signed ABN is on file. | **Billable to patient.** | [CMS Pub 100-04 transmittal R2148CP](https://www.cms.gov/Regulations-and-Guidance/Guidance/Transmittals/downloads/R2148CP.pdf) |
| Medicare **statutorily excluded** service (modifier **GY**) | Denied with CARC **96** + RARC **N425** "Statutorily excluded service(s)". Item is excluded by statute and is not a Medicare benefit; a **voluntary** ABN may be issued — a mandatory ABN is not required to hold the beneficiary liable. | **Billable to patient** (benefit exclusion, not a coverage judgment). | [Noridian, Reason Code 96 / Remark Code N425](https://med.noridianmedicare.com/web/jddme/topics/ra/denial-resolution/n425-96); [Noridian, Services Excluded By Statute](https://med.noridianmedicare.com/web/jea/topics/abn/services-excluded-by-statute) |

## 4. The appeal-vs-resubmit gate (Medicare FFS) — check this before choosing a remedy

| Signal on the 835 | What it means | Correct disposition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| RARC **MA130** present | Claim was **returned as unprocessable**, not denied. "Your claim contains incomplete and/or invalid information, and no appeal rights are afforded because the claim is unprocessable. Please submit a new claim with the complete/correct information." Contractors "must return the unprocessable claim through the remittance process, **without offering appeal rights**" and "shall not deny claims and afford appeal rights for incomplete or invalid information." | **Correct and submit a NEW claim.** Do **not** file a redetermination; an appeal will be dismissed. | [CMS transmittal R2041CP (Ch. 1 §80.3.2, unprocessable claims / MA130)](https://www.cms.gov/transmittals/downloads/r2041cp.pdf); [CMS, Claims Processing Manual Ch. 1](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c01.pdf) |
| MA130 **absent**, adjustment carries a substantive CARC | Claim was adjudicated and denied | Appeal (redetermination) or reopening, per [reference/appeal-levels.md](./appeal-levels.md) | [CMS, Claims Processing Manual Ch. 34 (Reopenings)](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c34.pdf) |

## 6. RARC table

RARCs never carry the adjustment themselves — a supplemental RARC explains a CARC, and an
"Alert:" RARC explains nothing about liability at all

## 7. Disposition summary, keyed by what you can observe

| Observed | Disposition | Never do |
|---|---|---|
| Any CARC + **MA130** (Medicare) | Correct, submit **new claim** | File a redetermination |
| CARC 16 / 206 / RARC M51, M76, N286, N290 | Data-defect repair + resubmit | Bill the patient; appeal a data defect |
| CARC 18 (OA) | No action if truly duplicate; else modifier/condition code + corrected line resubmission | Resubmit the entire claim unchanged |
| CARC 22, 109, RARC MA04 | Route to the correct payer / supply primary payer data | Appeal to the payer that told you it is not theirs |
| CARC 29 | Exception request if one of the four Medicare exceptions applies; else write off | Bill the patient for a late-filing write-off |
| CARC 45 (CO, partial) | Post contractual write-off | Treat a 100%-of-charge CO-45 as a legitimate write-off without challenge |
| CARC 50, 97, 151, B15 | Clinical/coding appeal with documentation, or corrected coding | Rebill MPFS status "B" codes; appeal MUE denials to the NCCI contractor |
| CARC 96/204 with **CO** | Write off | Bill the patient |
| CARC 96/204/27/50 with **PR** | Bill the patient **after** clearing §3 overrides (QMB, Medicaid payment-in-full, NSA, ABN validity) | Bill a QMB for Part A/B cost sharing |
| Any adjustment with **PI** | Appeal — payer asserts a reduction with no contract behind it | Auto-write-off |
| CARC 181, 197, B7 | Fix the underlying registry/enrolment/authorisation fact, then resubmit or request reopening | Appeal before the enrolment/auth record is corrected |

## Open questions

- **Verbatim X12 text and effective dates.** `x12.org` was unreachable from this environment, so the "Notes:" clauses and the Start/Stop/Last-Modified dates on the live CARC and RARC lists could not be read directly. Wording above is taken from CMS transmittals, CMS manual attachments, MAC pages, state Medicaid and DoD republications of the X12 lists. Where a runtime decision turns on exact wording, re-read [x12.org/codes/claim-adjustment-reason-codes](https://x12.org/codes/claim-adjustment-reason-codes) and [x12.org/codes/remittance-advice-remark-codes](https://x12.org/codes/remittance-advice-remark-codes).
- **Group-code usage restrictions not confirmed for CARC 22, 109, 204, 206.** Restrictions of the form "(Use only with Group Code X)" were confirmed only for **18** (OA, with a workers'-comp CO exception) and **45** (PR or CO). Searches for a comparable restriction on 22, 109, 204 and 206 returned nothing authoritative. Do not assert a required group code for those four.
- **"Usage: Refer to the 835 Healthcare Policy Identification Segment (loop 2110 Service Payment Information REF), if present."** This note is widely reproduced against CARCs 50, 97, 151, 197, 204, B7 and B15 in secondary sources; it could not be confirmed per-code from a primary source and is therefore not asserted per-code above.
- **RARC M80 wording conflict.** A CMS-sourced retrieval gives "We cannot pay for this when performed during the same session as another approved service for this beneficiary", while secondary sources give "Not covered when performed during the same session/date as a previously processed service for the patient." The conflict is unresolved; the X12 list is controlling.
- **RARC N448 and N130 exact current text** were retrieved from state-Medicaid republications and secondary lists, not from a primary read of the X12 list. Whether N448 is classed supplemental or informational was not established.
- **QMB RARC N783** could not be confirmed. **N781** (deductible) and **N782** (coinsurance) are confirmed by CMS; the existence/text of a third QMB RARC is unverified.
- **CORE-required code combinations** (which CAGC/CARC/RARC triplets a HIPAA-covered plan may send for each of the four CORE denial business scenarios) were not retrieved. The rule and scenario names are cited; the combination tables themselves are a separate CAQH CORE companion document updated three times a year and should be pulled directly before relying on combination-level logic.
- **Patient-billing rules for CARC 197 (no prior authorisation) outside Medicare.** Whether a commercial plan may shift a no-auth denial to the patient depends on state law, plan documents and the provider contract. No general rule was established; do not infer one.
- **Medicaid timely-filing limits and exception processes** are state-specific and were not researched; the 1-calendar-year rule and its four exceptions cited above are **Medicare FFS only**.
- **Medicaid and commercial use of CARC 96 vs 204** for benefit exclusions was not differentiated from primary sources; the Medicare statutory-exclusion pairing (96 + N425 + GY) does not transfer to other payer types.
