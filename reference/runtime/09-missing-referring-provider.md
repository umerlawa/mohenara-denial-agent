# Missing / invalid referring provider (CO-16 + N286)

**Recognise:** remit_835 carries CARC **16** under group **CO** with MOA remark **N286** — the corpus signature (13 cases). CARC 16 ("lacks information or has submission/billing error(s)… at least one Remark Code must be provided") is **not actionable alone; the paired RARC names the defect**. N286 = "Missing/incomplete/invalid referring provider primary identifier": the ordering/referring NPI is absent, mismatched to the name, or the provider is not in **PECOS** / not of a specialty eligible to order or refer. Check the claim's referring name + NPI against the NPI Registry and PECOS. Neighbours: **206** (NPI missing from any required loop), **N290** (*rendering* provider), **B7** (ordering/referring enrolled but ineligible or not effective on the DOS). **MA130 appears in zero corpus cases** — look anyway: MA130, or any outcome with no 835, means unprocessable, no appeal rights, new claim only.

**Disposition:** `correct_and_resubmit`. Fix the ordering/referring name + NPI and resubmit. This is CORE scenario 2 (missing/invalid **data from the submitted claim**) → rework; scenario 1 (missing **documentation**) → appeal, which this is not (denial-lifecycle.md §3.3; CAQH CORE 360). Medicare Part B FFS accepts only `CLM05-3 = 1`; commercial/Medicaid replacement = `7` + `REF*F8` from 835 `CLP07`. Defect surfaced on a 277CA/999 rejection with no 835 → new `1`.

**Do not:** do not `appeal` — the determination is right for what was billed, and appealing a data defect burns the window. Do not `bill_patient`: group CO, never patient responsibility. Do not send `7` to Medicare Part B (rejection, not correction), and never run an appeal and a corrected claim on the same DOS/CPT pair.

Uncertain: which 837P loop carries the **referring** provider is established nowhere in the research — only Loop 2310B / Item 24J for the *rendering* provider (N290) is sourced; do not assert a loop number. What a 277CA says for this defect is also unsourced: only categories A3 and A7 ("Rejected for Invalid Information") are confirmed, and the 277CA is Medicare-FFS practice, not HIPAA-mandated. **Unresolved on Part B:** the N286 row says resubmit as a new claim, while denial-lifecycle.md §4 row 1a routes a Part B provider-side data defect to a **clerical-error reopening** — not a replacement, not an appeal. The MA130 gate that would force "new claim" never fires here and no file gives a fallback for "Part B, data defect, no MA130"; if the claim adjudicated (835 with CLP07), reopening vs new claim is open — consider `awaiting_human`. Group-code restrictions were confirmed only for CARC 18 and 45, so do not assert that 16 must be CO.
